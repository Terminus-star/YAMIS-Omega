# YAMIS-Omega

Modified fork of **YAMIS** (yet-another-monochrome-icon-set) by Dirn, https://bitbucket.org/dirn-typo/yet-another-monochrome-icon-set, licensed GPL-3.0 (see LICENSE, Authors and changelog, kept unchanged).

Change: every `currentColor` fill and stroke is replaced by a vertical gradient in the Mass Effect Omega palette (#FBEFDF to #C98A4E; accent parts #C9F28F to #A2E75D), generated by `theme/gen_icons.py` in the mass-effect-hud project (https://github.com/Terminus-star/mass-effect-hud). Icons that carry their own colours are unchanged. This variant is designed for dark surfaces.

Also added, in YAMIS's own flat style before this recolour, then recoloured the same as everything else:
- `apps/scalable/matlab.svg` - MATLAB, from the Papirus icon theme, background tile removed.
- `apps/scalable/euro-office-lite.svg` - Euro Office Lite, redrawn from hicolor's raster icon at measured coordinates, background tile removed.

(`zero-trust-orange.svg`, Cloudflare One Client, was already covered upstream and is unchanged here except for this recolour.)
